# Governance

SignalQuarry is led by its maintainer, Pengyuan Li, who has final say on
scope, design and releases (a benevolent-dictator model suited to a small
project). Decisions are recorded as ADRs in `docs/adr/`. Areas with extra
review (see `.github/CODEOWNERS`): validation gates, the paper kernel,
schemas, project templates and the leak scan.

If the project gains regular maintainers, this document will describe how
they are added and how decisions are made.
